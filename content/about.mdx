I'm a Product Designer based in Singapore with a background in architecture and two years of experience designing digital products for government and enterprise.

My architecture training gave me an instinct for systems thinking, spatial logic, and designing for people across very different contexts. I bring that same rigour to digital products — whether I'm mapping end-to-end service journeys, facilitating research with users who aren't tech-savvy, or working through the constraints of a complex public sector environment.

I'm currently looking for product design roles where I can work on meaningful, complex problems — particularly in public sector, government-adjacent organisations, or AI-native products.

**Tools:** Figma, FigJam, Miro, Hotjar, Adobe Creative Suite
